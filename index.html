Superfast Doorstep Delivery

Get hot, aromatic biryani and chicken lollipops delivered directly to your doorstep in Pamuru. We maintain high hygienic standards during delivery, ensuring food reaches you just as if it came out of the kitchen.

Passing by Nellore Road? Don't even get out of your vehicle. Drive into our dedicated drive-through pickup lane, scan your order QR code or give your order number, and our staff will load the food directly into your vehicle.
